# Name and logo

The code is free to use, change and share under the GPL-3.0-or-later (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The **name** "Minimal Fitness",
the **app icon** and the **logo** are not licensed for reuse. This follows section 7 of the GPL, which lets a project keep its
trademark rights.

What this means in practice:

* You may fork the code, build it and publish your version. Please give it **your own name and icon** so people can tell the
  two apart, and say that it is based on Minimal Fitness.
* You may say that your version is "based on Minimal Fitness" or "a fork of Minimal Fitness".
* Please do not publish a modified build under the name "Minimal Fitness" or with the same icon, and do not suggest it is the
  original or endorsed by the original author.
* Rebuilds you make for yourself, or unmodified builds, are fine.

This is a request about names, not a limit on the code: nothing here restricts what the GPL already lets you do with the source.
